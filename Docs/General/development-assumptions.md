# Development Assumptions

> **Binding agent rules.** A player-visible decision the [design](../Design/README.md) does not settle is registered here
> before it ships, and its id (`DA-n`) is cited in the commit footer and the docs that describe the behaviour; code has no comments.

Each entry: the decision, why it was needed, what would change if it is decided otherwise, and
its status (`Open`, `Confirmed <date>`, `Withdrawn <date>`).

| DA-22 | Pausing, winning or failing lets go of a held block (it settles on the cell the board has) and ignores the finger until the level starts or resumes; restart is allowed while playing, paused or failed, not after a win | The design lists pause, restart and fail but not what happens to a block in the hand, nor whether a won level can restart | Keep the held block across a pause; allow restart from the win popup | Open |
| DA-23 | Until the win popup exists, winning starts the next level by itself one second later (long enough to watch the last block leave) | Levels must chain to be played and tested before T-21 | T-21 replaces the timed start with the popup's Continue button | Withdrawn 2026-11-17 (the win popup's Continue starts the next level) |
| DA-24 | Every door carries one arrow on top, centred however wide the door is, pointing out of the board; it sinks with the door | The reference shows arrows on doors (deconstruction.md) but only on one-cell doors | One arrow per door cell | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-25 | Online is decided by a `GET` to a probe URL that answers 204 with a `Date` header (`https://www.google.com/generate_204` until the game has its own server), 5 s timeout; re-probed every 10 s online, every 3 s offline and at once on app resume; one failed probe means offline | The developer asked for an online-only game; there is no backend yet | Point `NetworkConfig.ProbeUrl` at our own server; require two failures before going offline if the popup flickers on bad networks | Open |
| DA-26 | While offline in gameplay the level timer stands still, a held block is dropped and touches are ignored; the level is not paused and nothing is lost; play continues where it stopped once online | The developer asked that the timer and input stop under the popup; the design has no offline case | Pause the level (pause popup under it) or fail it after a grace period | Open |
| DA-27 | Trusted time is the last successful probe's `Date` header plus the monotonic time since; before the first success it is unavailable and features that need it wait | Wall-clock features must not trust the device clock (R-44) | Use our own server's time endpoint with sub-second precision | Open |
| DA-28 | The gameplay HUD follows the reference screen: level badge over a time panel at the top left, restart and pause buttons and the coin counter at the top right, four booster buttons at the bottom; lives are not shown in gameplay | The design lists the HUD's parts but not their layout; the reference HUD shows no lives | Move parts in the scene; show lives in the HUD | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-29 | Pausing opens the settings popup: vibration, sound and music switches that work, Legal Terms, Restore Purchases, Support and Language placeholders (press feedback only), and Home; closing it resumes | The developer gave the reference settings popup as the pause popup | Add Resume and Restart buttons; wire the placeholders | Open |
| DA-30 | When the timer runs out, an "Out of Time!" popup offers 20 more seconds for 50 coins on the same board; without 50 coins the button cannot be pressed; no ads or offers | The developer asked for the reference out-of-time screen without the offer | Tune `GameplayConfig.ContinueSeconds` and `ContinueCost`; drop the continue | Withdrawn 2026-11-17: the developer removed the continue; running out of time ends the attempt (DA-31) |
| DA-31 | Running out of time ends the attempt at once: one "Level Failed!" popup showing the heart with "-1", no paid continue; Retry restarts the level when a life is left and otherwise opens the lives popup; Return to Menu and the close button go Home; no boosters; the failure takes the attempt's life (DA-38) | The developer asked for a single fail popup that restarts or goes Home, keeping the reference failed screen with its middle left empty | Bring back a paid continue before the failed popup | Open |
| DA-32 | A level counts as solvable when some order of moves clears it with the game's own rules: a move takes one block along any path of free cells (the drag allows that), and a block that can reach its door leaves at once; the solver searches board states up to a cap and says "unknown" past it | The design asks for an unsolvable-level warning without saying what a move is | Count only straight slides as moves | Open |
| DA-33 | The generator puts one door per colour on the walls, fills the board with preset-shaped blocks of those colours, and keeps only valid, solvable levels; a level's difficulty is its solution's exit waves plus room-making moves: Normal 1–2, Hard 3–4, Very Hard 5 or more (the shipped levels score 1, 2, 4, 4, 8); the timer is 20 s plus 10 s per block, plus 30 % for Hard and 60 % for Very Hard | The developer asked for generation by difficulty without a difficulty measure | Change `LevelGenerator.ScoreFor` and `TimerFor` | Open |
| DA-34 | Editor shortcuts: 1–4 tools, Q / E colour, Tab shape, R rotate, I / Shift+I ice, A arrow, H make harder, arrows move, Delete remove, Esc deselect, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo, Ctrl+S save, N new, Shift+D copy, [ ] previous / next level, C check, Space step the solution, G generate, P play, F1 help; plain letters, so they never clash with Unity's own Ctrl shortcuts | The developer asked for shortcuts for every tool | Rebind in `LevelEditorWindow.HandleShortcuts` | Open |
| DA-36 | Boosters: a new player owns 20 of each (developer, 2026-11-18); one more costs Freeze 40, Rocket 60, Hammer 80, Vacuum 120 coins; freeze stops the timer for 10 s of play, waits for the first grab when used before it (developer, 2026-11-17) and does not stack; rocket, hammer and vacuum wait for a tap on a block while the timer keeps running and dragging is off (an empty cell does nothing, tapping the booster again puts it back; developer, 2026-11-17); the rocket takes one cell, and when the rest falls apart every piece but the biggest becomes a block of its own (same colour and ice) that moves and leaves by itself; the vacuum takes every block of the tapped colour; a booster is spent only when it works; with none left a tap pauses and offers one for coins, and buying does not use it | The developer asked for the four boosters of the reference with no stock, prices or edge cases given | Change `BoosterConfig`; the rules live in `BoosterService`, the booster commands and `LevelTimer.FreezeFor` | Open |
| DA-37 | Ice blocker: a frozen block shows a number and cannot move; every block that leaves the board (through a door or taken by a booster) counts every frozen block down by one, and at 0 it thaws; the hammer breaks the ice instead of the block; the rocket and the vacuum treat a frozen block like any other; a level may not give a block more ice than it has other blocks | T-34 says "thaws after N exits" only | Change `BoardState.MarkGone`, `HammerBoosterCommand` and the validator's ice rule | Open |
| DA-38 | Lives: 5 at most, a new player is full; a missing life comes back after 30 minutes of trusted time, one countdown at a time, also while the app is closed; an attempt costs one life only when the player started it (first grab) and abandons it (runs out of time, restarts, or leaves for Home), never more than one, and a win costs none; playing with no lives opens a popup with the next life's countdown and a refill of every life for 100 coins; the HUD shows no lives | The developer asked for the usual casual lives with a refill offer for coins, no numbers given | Change `LivesConfig`; the rules live in `LivesService` and `LevelAttemptLives` | Open |
| DA-39 | Arrow block: a block with an arrow moves, and leaves, only along its axis (horizontal: left and right, out through the left or right wall; vertical: up and down, out through the bottom or top); it can still be picked up, the drag does not even lean across its axis, it never lets go, it can carry ice too, and a piece the rocket splits off keeps the arrow; the arrow is a white double-headed arrow sized to the block (1, 2 or 3+ cells) | The developer showed reference blocks that go one way only and asked for them as a blocker | Change `ArrowBlocker` and the validator's door rule | Open |
| DA-40 | The catalog has 20 levels. Levels 1–8 keep their gentle ramp; from level 9 they come in groups of six that each end on a peak, with one breather (a Hard level) third in the group. Hard levels come from the level hardener, which climbs a generated level toward more room-making moves with arrows and ice. A level's timer is the generator's suggestion plus 15 s for every room-making move, rounded to 5 s and capped at the rules' maximum | The developer asked for 20 levels on a mixed curve whose hard levels are real puzzles | Change the curve in `ShippedLevelsTests` and rebuild the levels with the hardener | Open |
| DA-41 | A level pack from remote config plays from the next launch, never mid-session; it must pass the validator and have a revision no lower than the bundled pack's, otherwise the bundled levels play; progress stays a level number, which loops over a shorter pack | The developer asked for levels changeable later through remote config | Change `LevelPackService.LoadAsync` / `TryStoreRemote` | Open |
| DA-42 | Low-time warning: from 10 s left down to 1 s, every second the timer flashes red and swells and a tick plays; nothing flashes or ticks while paused or frozen, and a new start shows the normal timer | The developer asked for a ticking, flashing timer at the end and chose 10 s | Change the low-time fields of `GameplayConfig` | Open |
| DA-43 | Sound: one short effect each for a button tap, a block grab, a block leaving through its door, a booster use, a win and a fail, plus the low-time tick; clips are synthesized by `tools/synth_sfx.py` (the developer chose code-made clips over AI-generated ones); the sound toggle mutes every effect at once, the music toggle has no music to control yet | The developer asked for sounds in the usual places and chose synthesized clips | Swap the clips in `AudioConfig`; add music behind the music toggle | Open |
