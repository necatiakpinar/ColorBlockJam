# Development Assumptions

> **Binding agent rules.** A player-visible decision the [design](../Design/README.md) does not settle is registered here
> before it ships, and its id (`DA-n`) is cited in the code comment and the commit footer.

Each entry: the decision, why it was needed, what would change if it is decided otherwise, and
its status (`Open`, `Confirmed <date>`, `Withdrawn <date>`).

| Id | Decision | Why | If decided otherwise | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DA-1 | Portrait orientation, 1080x1920 canvas reference | Mobile casual default | Change Player orientation and every CanvasScaler | Confirmed 2026-11-16 (R-2) |
| DA-2 | Level numbers are 1-based and persist locally (PlayerPrefs) | Needed to show "Level N" before level progression exists | Swap `IKeyValueStorage` implementation; `PlayerProgressService` unchanged | Open |
| DA-3 | Splash stays up at least 1.5 s | Avoids a flash on fast devices | Tune `SplashConfig.MinimumDisplaySeconds` | Open |
| DA-4 | Controls: one finger drags one block; it follows the finger freely like a solid object (diagonals included), its drawing gliding after the finger over about 0.12 s instead of jumping, slides along walls and blocks instead of passing through them, slips into a one-cell gap when slightly off its line, leans a little toward the finger when it has no room, shows an outline while held, and on release settles on the nearest cell with a short tween | The design names the genre but not its rules | `BlockDragController` and `BoardState.TryStep` change; model tests rewritten | Open |
| DA-5 | A block exits when it is pushed against a door of its colour whose span covers the block's whole span on that wall and nothing stands between the block and that wall; the level is won when every block has exited | Exit and win rules are not written in the design | `DoorResolver` and `BoardState.IsCleared` change | Open |
| DA-6 | The timer shows the full time when the level appears and starts counting at the player's first grab of a block; it freezes while paused or the app is backgrounded, and fails the level at 0 | R-18 says only "counts down and stops the level at zero" | `LevelTimer` start trigger changes (e.g. when the level appears) | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-7 | Coins are earned only by winning, by the level's difficulty: Normal 10, Hard 25, Very Hard 50 (`LevelRewardConfig`), credited before the win popup; the starting balance is 0 | R-19 defines no economy; the developer asked for rewards by difficulty | Change the table in `LevelRewardConfig`; wallet and service unchanged | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-8 | After the last catalog level, levels loop from level 1 while the shown level number keeps rising | Only 5 levels exist | `LevelProvider` mapping changes | Open |
| DA-9 | Lives are a static placeholder: the heart shows a full count and never changes | R-23: the life system is a placeholder | Add a lives service; HUD and Home bind it | Open |
| DA-10 | Settings toggles persist locally and only change their own visual state; nothing plays audio, and the haptic taps (DA-21) are not gated by the vibration toggle yet | R-14: settings buttons are placeholders; the art set has no audio | Audio and haptic services read `ISettingsService` | Open |
| DA-11 | Tapping a tab gives press feedback only; the Home tab stays active and no content changes | R-8: the tabs are placeholders | Tab bar moves the `ActiveTab` highlight | Open |
| DA-12 | Winning shows a win popup with the reward and one Continue button that loads the next level | R-24 defines no screen for continuing | Add a Home button to the win popup | Open |
| DA-13 | Levels are `LevelDefinition` ScriptableObject assets listed in one `LevelCatalog`; the editor writes them and the game reads them | R-29: one saved format; designers work in Unity | Swap to JSON files behind a loader; editor save path changes | Open |
| DA-14 | Leaving a level through Home discards the attempt with no penalty; coins and level stay as before the attempt | The design does not define abandoning | Add a life cost or a confirmation dialog | Open |
| DA-15 | Restart (from pause or fail) costs nothing and resets the board and timer | Lives are a placeholder | Restart consumes a life once DA-9 is replaced | Open |
| DA-16 | Boosters show four buttons (hammer, vacuum, rocket, freeze) with press feedback and no effect | "Boosters do not need a function" | T-36 makes one functional | Open |
| DA-17 | Doors sit on the outer wall ring only, never on corners; one colour per door; length 1 to the wall length | Door rules are not written | Editor door tool and `DoorData` change | Open |
| DA-18 | A block is one colour and any edge-connected set of cells (polyomino) | Block shapes are not listed | Editor block tool and validator change | Open |
| DA-19 | The board camera looks down steeply (72° tilt, 30° field of view) so blocks read mostly from the top, and the held block's outline traces its on-screen silhouette (bottom edge on the sides the camera sees, top rim on the others) | The design does not set the camera angle or the outline's look | Retune the Gameplay scene camera; `BoardFraming` refits any tilt | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-20 | A block leaves as soon as it is pushed 0.15 cell into its own door; it settles at the door's mouth (0.08 s) and only then slides into it at full size at a steady 6 cells per second, cut away at the board's edge, while cubes of its colour burst out of the door in a cone pointing away from the board | The design says blocks leave through doors but not how it looks | Tune `ExitSpeed`, `ExitDebrisBurst`, `ExitDebrisRate` and `BlockExitBurst.prefab` | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-21 | Feel polish: a press up to 0.35 cell next to a block picks it; a door a block cannot use (another colour, or too narrow) is a plain wall to it, with no shake or haptic; a leaving block gives a light haptic tap; a door sinks into the floor, whole, while a block passes and rises after; blocks leaving one door within 0.15 s share one debris spray | The design does not describe touch tolerance, feedback at a wrong door or door motion | Tune `TouchTolerance`, the door gate and coalesce settings in `BoardViewConfig` | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-22 | Pausing, winning or failing lets go of a held block (it settles on the cell the board has) and ignores the finger until the level starts or resumes; restart is allowed while playing, paused or failed, not after a win | The design lists pause, restart and fail but not what happens to a block in the hand, nor whether a won level can restart | Keep the held block across a pause; allow restart from the win popup | Open |
| DA-23 | Until the win popup exists, winning starts the next level by itself one second later (long enough to watch the last block leave) | Levels must chain to be played and tested before T-21 | T-21 replaces the timed start with the popup's Continue button | Open |
| DA-24 | Every door carries one arrow on top, centred however wide the door is, pointing out of the board; it sinks with the door | The reference shows arrows on doors (deconstruction.md) but only on one-cell doors | One arrow per door cell | Confirmed 2026-11-17 (developer) |
| DA-25 | Online is decided by a `GET` to a probe URL that answers 204 with a `Date` header (`https://www.google.com/generate_204` until the game has its own server), 5 s timeout; re-probed every 10 s online, every 3 s offline and at once on app resume; one failed probe means offline | The developer asked for an online-only game; there is no backend yet | Point `NetworkConfig.ProbeUrl` at our own server; require two failures before going offline if the popup flickers on bad networks | Open |
| DA-26 | While offline in gameplay the level timer stands still, a held block is dropped and touches are ignored; the level is not paused and nothing is lost; play continues where it stopped once online | The developer asked that the timer and input stop under the popup; the design has no offline case | Pause the level (pause popup under it) or fail it after a grace period | Open |
| DA-27 | Trusted time is the last successful probe's `Date` header plus the monotonic time since; before the first success it is unavailable and features that need it wait | Wall-clock features must not trust the device clock (R-44) | Use our own server's time endpoint with sub-second precision | Open |
